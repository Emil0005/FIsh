THE PRODUCER JOURNEY

How We Grow Our Fish

Unlike trading companies that purchase from anonymous third-party sources, BRINQ controls every phase of life. From egg incubation in cold springs to processing beside our pools, our closed-loop farm guarantees unmatched taste and firm texture.

01
PRISTINE WATER SOURCE

Glacial Mountain Springs

Our pools receive an uninterrupted flow of natural high-altitude mountain water. Constant cold temperatures (8°C – 12°C year-round) provide peak natural dissolved oxygen.

  • Naturally filtered through ancient volcanic basalt rock
  • Gravity-fed continuous fresh flow (no recycled water)
  • Zero industrial or agricultural runoff
02
ETHICAL REARING

Hatchery & Slow Natural Maturation

We incubate select roe in our own mountain hatchery. Fish grow slowly against strong water currents, developing firm, resilient muscle tone without excessive soft belly fat.

  • Low stocking density for stress-free swimming
  • Active muscle development against natural currents
  • Certified pedigree mountain trout (Karmrakhayt)
03
100% CLEAN NUTRITION

Certified Eco-Nutrition & Zero Additives

Our fish are fed only non-GMO, certified sustainable marine and plant proteins rich in natural astaxanthin and EPA/DHA Omega-3s. We never use antibiotics or growth hormones.

  • 100% Non-GMO, fully traceable feed ingredients
  • Natural vibrant coral-pink flesh color
  • Clean, subtly sweet flavor without earthy pond smell
04
AT-SOURCE PACKING

Humane Harvest & Immediate Sealing

Our packing facility sits directly adjacent to our spring pools. Cold-harvested fish are processed, hand-trimmed, and sealed under thermoform vacuum in under 3 hours.

  • Hermetic skin-pack trays locking in cellular juices
  • Flash-frozen at source or chilled for live-fresh export
  • Zero delay between swimming and sealing
